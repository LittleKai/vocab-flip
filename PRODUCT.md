# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Language learners, students, and professionals who need a reliable, efficient tool for vocabulary memorization and flashcard creation. They study in varying environments (daylight, nighttime), requiring a focused and distraction-free interface.

## Product Purpose
A multi-language vocabulary learning flashcard app (VocabFlip) that allows users to create, manage, study, and sync flashcards. Success looks like users spending less time navigating the app and more time actively learning, with clear visual cues aiding their study sessions.

## Brand Personality
Modern, clear, and slightly vibrant but restrained. The interface feels structured and professional, using deliberate pops of color to guide attention without becoming a kaleidoscope.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, overly colorful layouts that distract from the learning content.
- Monotone or overly gray interfaces that feel lifeless.
- Color palettes that break or lose contrast when switching between light and dark modes.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over decoration:** Every UI element must have a clear functional purpose. If it doesn't help the user study or navigate, remove it.
- **Adaptive contrast:** Colors must be deliberately chosen to work perfectly and maintain a ≥4.5:1 contrast ratio in both Light and Dark themes.
- **Intentional vibrancy:** Keep the overall canvas neutral and clean, using a single or double accent color to highlight primary actions and important states (e.g., correct/incorrect).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for legibility in both light and dark modes (WCAG AA).
- Clear, readable typography (avoiding excessively thin weights).
- Touch targets must be generous enough for mobile use.
